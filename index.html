01 / About me

Rigorous analysis.
Meaningful insights.

I am a statistics-trained professional and a Master’s student in Statistics and Data Science at IPB University. My experience spans statistical modeling, data science, machine learning, natural language processing, quantitative research, official statistics, financial data, and university teaching.

I use R and Python to turn complex data into interpretable evidence, analytical reports, dashboards, presentations, and practical recommendations. My applied interests include public health, food security, spatial statistics, multivariate analysis, and Indonesian text analytics.

I am also interested in business analytics, statistical consulting, data engineering, ETL workflows, database systems, and reliable data pipelines.
